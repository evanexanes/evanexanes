## What I do

- **WordPress and Elementor builds:** templates that someone on your team can fill in, built with Elementor and ACF.
- **Hand-written HTML and CSS:** for pages that have to be exact. Layouts that hold at 360 pixels and stay readable when a client pastes in twice as much text as planned.
- **Technical SEO and structured data:** JSON-LD so search engines and AI assistants can read what each page is, slugs audited before a rename, and redirect chains collapsed to one hop.
